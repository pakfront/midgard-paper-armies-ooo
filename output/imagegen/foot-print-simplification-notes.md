# Simplified Rohan and Isengard foot artwork

Edited with the built-in image generation tool following `clean-style-tests-notes.md`. All four catalog filenames remain unchanged. Each rank retains eight figures, complete equipment, faction colors and transparent backgrounds. Fine mail, texture and ornament were replaced by broad shapes and stronger contours for 40 mm frontage printing.

+## Rohan/rohan-foot-archers.png

Edit target and saved asset: `artwork/middle-earth-third-age/Rohan/rohan-foot-archers.png`.

Use case: style-transfer. Edit target is the supplied unit artwork. Simplify aggressively for a miniature paper standee printed only 40mm wide, bodies about 10mm high. Preserve exactly eight complete frontal figures, their poses, equipment, faction identity and palette, one compact horizontal rank. Redraw as CLEAN simplified ink illustration with bold crisp dark contours, broad smooth flat color masses, ONLY 2-3 shading tones per material. Remove ALL chainmail rings (replace by smooth slate-grey shapes), grain, mottling, scratches, stipple, crosshatching, hair strands, tiny buckles, rivets, embroidery and ornate trim. Minimal readable faces, hair in solid masses, robust weapon silhouettes, simple thick heraldic symbols. Clear smooth edges with gentle antialiasing, no jagged pixel art. Complete boots and all weapon tips inside frame with transparent padding on every edge. Genuine transparent RGBA background, no scenery, ground, shadows, text or borders. Preserve wide landscape layout. Eight Rohan foot archers with wooden longbows at rest and quivers; green cloaks, brown leather, silver helmets, restrained gold. Remove tiny horse trim entirely.
