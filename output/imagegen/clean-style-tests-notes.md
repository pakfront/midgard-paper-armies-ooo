# Clean infantry artwork

Prompts for the catalog artwork `gondor-swordsmen-clean.png` and `orc-axemen-clean.png`, generated with the built-in image generation tool. Use the workshop to export printable PDFs at 100% / actual size. Earlier print tests and frontal cavalry artwork are available in Git history.

## Prompts

### gondor-swordsmen-clean

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE unit for a miniature printable paper standee. Reference 1 establishes the required CLEAN simplified style: bold crisp ink contours, broad smooth watercolor-like color masses, just 2-3 shading tones, highly distinct materials. No grain, mottling, scratches, stipple, crosshatching, tiny rings, fur strands or fine detail. Reference 2 establishes Gondor armor and white tree heraldry only; remove its texture. Straight-on frontal orthographic elevation, complete bodies, feet and weapon tips. Compact overlapping connected horizontal silhouette, transparent RGBA background with clean padding; no scenery, ground, shadow, base, text or border. Intended unit width 40mm, infantry bodies ~10mm high; simplify aggressively for small print. Eight Gondor swordsmen in an orderly line, bright cool silver-blue plate and helmets, charcoal black cloth, warm natural human faces, brown leather. Broad black shields with simple bold white tree symbols with few thick branches. Upright swords close to bodies, subtle varied faces and poses. Match the reference orcs' clean readable rendering, not their anatomy or gear.

### orc-axemen-clean

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE unit for a miniature printable paper standee. Reference 1 establishes the required CLEAN simplified style: bold crisp ink contours, broad smooth watercolor-like color masses, just 2-3 shading tones, highly distinct materials. No grain, mottling, scratches, stipple, crosshatching, tiny rings, fur strands or fine detail. Reference 2 establishes Gondor armor and white tree heraldry only; remove its texture. Straight-on frontal orthographic elevation, complete bodies, feet and weapon tips. Compact overlapping connected horizontal silhouette, transparent RGBA background with clean padding; no scenery, ground, shadow, base, text or border. Intended unit width 40mm, infantry bodies ~10mm high; simplify aggressively for small print. Eight DIFFERENT orc axemen and cleaver fighters in an irregular ragged warband with uneven head heights and varied builds. New poses and equipment, not a copy of reference line. Clearly grassy green skin with lighter yellow-green highlights, cool slate-blue steel, brick red and ochre cloth, chocolate brown leather. Mix bare heads, crude helmets, hoods, round and angular shields, robust axes and cleavers held close. Keep faces minimal and clean and all surfaces almost untextured.
