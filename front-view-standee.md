# Front-view standee figure prompt

Attach the figures you want to match as reference images. Replace the bracketed fields, then paste the prompt below into your image generator. This prompt uses the collection's pencil, ink and watercolour style with a front-facing camera for upright paper standees.

## Prompt

Create **ONE complete, isolated, front-facing figure for a printable paper standee**: **[SUBJECT / FACTION / ROLE]**.

Use the supplied images as references for illustration style, character design, clothing, equipment and palette. Reinterpret the subject as a full-length frontal illustration. The reference camera angle must not carry over.

**View and pose:** straight-on front elevation at the subject's chest height, orthographic appearance, no camera tilt or perspective distortion. Face, chest, hips and feet face the viewer. Show the complete body from the top of the headgear to the bottoms of both feet. Use believable anatomy and a balanced, grounded stance with feet on the same baseline. Keep the face and main equipment clearly readable. Pose: **[E.G. SPEAR HELD UPRIGHT BESIDE THE BODY, SHIELD AT THE OTHER SIDE]**. Keep weapons mainly in the picture plane so their shapes remain recognizable; avoid weapons pointing toward the viewer and extreme foreshortening.

**Illustration style:** expressive graphite pencil and dark sepia ink, slightly broken hand-drawn contours, sparse construction strokes and selective cross-hatching, finished with restrained translucent watercolour washes. Realistic historical or fantasy illustration matching the references. Use clear silhouettes, broad readable shading and a few structural cloth folds. Suggest mail with dark steel shading instead of drawing every ring. Keep faces simple but legible. Preserve the handmade character at small print size.

**Design:** **[CLOTHING, ARMOUR, HELMET, WEAPONS, SHIELD AND HERALDRY]**. Faction colours: **[LIVERY COLOURS]**. Keep steel, wood, leather, skin and animal coats naturally coloured. Preserve distinctive details from the reference unless explicitly replaced here.

**Standee suitability:** build a compact, connected silhouette that can be cut out of cardstock with a small surrounding margin. Keep arms, cloaks and equipment close enough to the body to avoid fragile narrow connections. Make long weapon shafts visually robust. Avoid tiny detached accessories, flying debris, loose effects and elaborate gaps that would require interior cutting. Keep the lower legs and feet unobstructed so the finished cutout can sit in a standee holder. Render the character artwork only; the physical holder and any assembly tabs will be added separately.

**Composition and output:** one figure centered on a portrait canvas, preferably 1024 × 1536 pixels or larger. Include every weapon tip, plume, shield edge, finger and foot, with generous clear padding on all sides. Genuine transparent RGBA background, clean antialiased edges and solid readable figure interiors; watercolour texture belongs within the figure. No scenery, ground patch, cast shadow, base, holder, paper rectangle, vignette, cut line, white sticker border, lettering or watermark. Deliver a transparent PNG.

**Exclude:** overhead or top-down view, elevated camera, isometric view, three-quarter view, profile, back view, cropped equipment, multiple figures, sprite sheets, photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, vector-flat cartoon styling, dense surface noise and a painted checkerboard background.

## Small-print detail control

Reduce high-frequency texture to limit aliasing at small print sizes. Render chain mail as smooth dark steel tonal masses with a few broad highlights, without individual rings, mesh, dots or repeated tiny marks. Use clean cloth washes and only a few structural folds; omit grain, speckling, weave and mottled surface noise. Simplify armour scratches, engraving and animal hair into broad readable shapes. Preserve crisp outer contours, equipment shapes and clear heraldry rather than blurring the finished artwork.

## Optional additions

- **For a mounted figure:** “One rider and one complete mount form a single standee. Both face directly toward the viewer. Show the mount from ears to hooves and the rider's visible head, torso, arms and equipment in correct anatomical positions. Keep the rider readable above the mount's head. Fit the entire subject and tack within the canvas.”
- **For a matching collection:** “Match the reference figure's body proportions, line weight and shading density. Keep the same camera height and frontal orientation. Vary the pose and equipment while retaining a coherent visual style.”
- **For a target print size:** “The intended humanoid height from soles to crown, excluding headgear and weapons, is **[HEIGHT IN MM]**. Simplify details for readability at that size.” Set the actual physical size in the print layout; image resolution alone does not determine it.

For a two-sided standee, create a separate rear-view illustration with the exact same outer silhouette, scale and foot baseline, then align the two sides in the print layout.
